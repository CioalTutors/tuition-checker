1. Overview

Welcome to CioalTutors ("we", "our", or "us"). We operate the Tuition Portal located at CioalTutors.com and related subdomains. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, protect, and handle your information when you access our portal and authenticate using Google Sign-In.

2. Information We Collect

When you sign in using your Google Account, we only collect minimal authentication data provided by Google:

  • Basic Profile Information: Your name and profile picture avatar.
  • Email Address: Used solely to verify your tutor identity and communicate regarding tuition placements.

We do NOT access your Gmail inbox, Google Drive, contacts, or any other personal Google data.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To verify registered tutors and grant secure access to tuition query details.
  • To pre-fill tuition application inquiries when you connect with our coordination team on WhatsApp.
  • To distinguish between verified teachers and platform administrators.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your authentication credentials and profile information are securely managed via Supabase with industry-standard encryption, Row Level Security (RLS) policies, and strict access controls. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

5. Third-Party Services

Our application interacts with the following trusted third-party providers:

  • Google OAuth 2.0: For secure teacher login authentication.
  • Supabase: For cloud database management and security rules.
  • WhatsApp: For direct communication between tutors and tuition coordinators.

6. User Rights & Data Deletion

You have the right to request deletion of your account and profile data at any time. To request data deletion, contact us at the email below.
